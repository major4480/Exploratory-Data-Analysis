# Exploratory-Data-Analysis
I performed Exploratory data analysis on the cleaned dataset of international layoff. I applied data manipulation and discovered some insightful results. Through these techniques, I was able to uncover meaningful patterns of global workforce reduction during COVID-19 from 2020-2023, highlighting trends across industries, companies and countries. 

## Results

I was able to show that United States had a lot of laid offs, more people lost their jobs during this period. I also showed that the year were most people were laid off was 2022, that is the year were a lot of people lost their jobs. And the company that had a lot of laid off was Amazon with 18150 people who lost their job. I performed some cool query of a rolling total to show the total number of people who were laid off during this period. I discovered that 383159 people were laid off during COVID time from March 2020 until March 2023.
